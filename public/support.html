Cinderbrook Brigade

A little help from the station.

This is a playable prototype. Production Google Play Games configuration and final store review are pending.

How to play

Drag one finger across the incident to aim the water jet. Lift to refill. Cool the hottest structures first and keep neighboring surfaces wet before the next ember arrives. Clearing every hotspot ends the incident; three stars reward a fast, careful rescue.

Returning to an incident

Your local save keeps campaign progress and an interrupted run. Returning to an active incident starts paused. Tap Resume when you are ready. Avoid clearing Android app storage unless you intend to discard local progress.

Ads and online features

This build uses Google test advertising. An optional emergency continue is granted only after the SDK reports that the reward was earned. If an ad is unavailable, you can retry normally. Web play supports local progression without native advertising or online Google Play Games.

Accessibility

Settings include separate music and effects, reduced motion and optional haptics. The aim indicator is offset above your finger to keep the target visible.

Report a problem

Use the public project issue tracker. Include the version, Android version or browser, incident name, expected result and observed result. Screenshots are helpful. Do not include private information or account credentials.
