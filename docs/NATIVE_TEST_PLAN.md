# Android acceptance run

Use the ordinary test APK with its exact SHA256 recorded. Keep test observations
distinct from automated model checks and scripted desktop scene fixtures. ADB
touch input is automated interaction through Android's normal touchscreen event
path; it is not human playtesting or a physical device.

1. Fresh launch: verify illustrated Home, readable controls, first gift, safe
   banner placement, one clear start action and no production service requests.
2. Core play: drag/hold over visible targets, observe jet/heat/steam and successful
   completion, then check stars, payout, XP, unlock and one-touch Next.
3. Refill: exhaust water, keep holding, confirm it stays empty; release to refill.
4. Failure: genuinely fail by time or structure, inspect opt-in continuation,
   ordinary retry/leave, and never grant a Web/unavailable/cancel reward.
5. Persistence: pause with Android Back, terminate/relaunch, verify the same
   incident and metrics without background time, then explicitly Resume.
6. Settlement: reload a completed result and verify no repeated currency/XP.
7. Progression: buy one affordable garage rank, verify its effect/cost and cold
   persistence; repeated input must not charge or grant unexpectedly.
8. Daily: verify claimed gift, no duplicate gift after reload, quests, seeded
   incident and legitimate once-per-date completion bonus.
9. Every area: Home, neighborhood map, garage, Daily, Endless Watch, journal,
   settings, privacy/service-unavailable handling, About/licenses, pause,
   success/failure and transition controls. Check scrolling and Android Back.
10. Ads: real test banner; every-third-round natural interstitial; earned reward
    plus dismissed/endcard path; unearned closure; persisted interruption before
    and after earned callback. Verify frozen world throughout and explicit Resume.
11. Native display profiles: phone 1080×1920, virtual 7-inch 1200×1920 and virtual
    10-inch 1600×2560. Use appropriate logical densities, preserve raw framebuffer
    pixels, inspect six distinct views per profile and restore original display.
12. Export/source identity: verify installed package/version and exact APK hash;
    bind all chosen screenshots to that APK and a public build receipt. Test
    final replacements after runtime changes, retaining older evidence honestly.

Use read-only emulator storage; preserve other applications and the owner's
editor. Record actual connectivity when testing offline behavior: toggling a
radio does not prove disconnection. No physical-device, subjective audio,
thermal, human-difficulty or production-backend acceptance is inferred.
