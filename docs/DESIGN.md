# Cinderbrook Brigade

The player sweeps a water jet over small burning places, then lifts their finger to refill. The miniature world supplies the spectacle; the readable heat/water tradeoff supplies the game. The full initial requirements are recorded before implementation in `INITIAL_VECTOR_PROMPT.txt`.

The playable board uses X/Z coordinates, a fixed oblique orthographic camera and 4–9 discrete vulnerable objects per authored incident. Heat is deterministic, moisture suppresses growth, damage comes from sustained fire, and only hot neighbors spread. Ember events are authored and telegraphed; preventive moisture can cancel them. A circular nozzle can cool several close objects at once. Touch aims slightly above the contact point so the visible ring remains legible. Releasing refills quickly; empty tank never traps the player.

Campaign has six districts, six individually named/layout-authored incidents each. Daily deterministically chooses an authored incident with small bounded modifiers. Endless Watch advances through authored incidents with increasing growth, then settles the full watch once. No random arrangement is described as authored.

Success awards supply coins, XP, a numeric score and one to three real icon stars. Three stars require at least 80% structure and 20% time remaining; two require at least 55% structure. An earned emergency continuation retains ordinary performance grading and can set competitive bests. Five upgrades have four ranks and explicit incremental effects; baseline gear remains sufficient for the entire campaign.

Profile version 1 uses a validated JSON primary, backup, staged write and monotonically increasing sequence. Active snapshots, settlement IDs and earned emergency grants are persisted. Every launch with an active run pauses for explicit Resume. Rewards are committed before presentation. Corrupt originals are preserved. Monotonic login/day keys prevent clock rollback duplication. Paused and background simulation receives no delta.

Monetization uses Google test IDs. Menu/result banners reserve space. Every third completed played round requests an interstitial. Only the SDK earned callback can persist an emergency grant; dismissal requires explicit Resume. Web has no pretend rewarded grants. Production Play IDs remain owner-supplied and disabled. The three board metrics are career XP, best incident score and highest Endless wave.

The visual bar is a detailed Kenney diorama with soft lighting, rich scenery, strong water/fire/steam contrast and original illustrated brand art. Assets, source provenance and model inspection are maintained in ASSET_AUDIT.md. Music differs across six districts. Reduced motion, sound, music and haptic switches persist.

Concept selection compared three directions: a vehicle-navigation fire brigade, a window-only hose puzzle, and a compact diorama with spreading heat. The diorama was selected because it has an immediately readable gesture, rich existing assets and a clear skill curve without driving or character-navigation friction. [Embr's official store listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1062830/Embr/) demonstrates the existing playful firefighting theme; [Firefighting Simulator: Ignite](https://www.firefighting-simulator.com/en/index.php) emphasizes authentic tools and crew simulation; [Firefighter Hose's developer listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.firefighter.hose.game.app) describes mobile touch aiming and window fires. Cinderbrook's distinction is miniature neighborhood layouts, heat spread, preventive wetness and quick release-to-refill rhythm. This comparison supports genre familiarity, not a sales or virality prediction. Sources reviewed 2026-11-14.

Implementation decisions after the initial vector: the initial proposed two-star limit after an ad continuation was superseded by the owner's preference for ordinary competitive grading after an earned assist. Final performance can receive three stars and set records. An invisible 38-pixel touch offset was also removed after input review: direct roof/ground hit targets plus a large visible spray-ring perimeter make aiming intuitive while leaving feedback visible around the finger. Original ScreenTouch controls are distinguished from Godot's emulated GUI mouse so a secondary finger cannot steal the hose.
