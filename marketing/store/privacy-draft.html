Cinderbrook Brigade

Privacy information

Prototype draft — not a release privacy policy. The developer must confirm their public identity, contact details, final SDK configuration and retention practices before release. This file is prepared locally and is not evidence of a hosted policy.

Progress stored on your device

The game stores campaign stars, upgrades, supply coins, career XP, local achievements, daily rewards, preferences and an interrupted incident on your device. The prototype does not require a developer account to play. Removing app data removes this local progress; make that choice only when you intend to reset the game.

Optional Google services

The Android prototype includes Google Mobile Ads with test IDs. Advertising SDKs may process device identifiers, interactions, diagnostics and IP-derived approximate location. The exact production declarations depend on the SDK version and final settings. This project does not claim that no data is collected. Google Play Games production sign-in, online achievements and leaderboards remain disabled until the owner supplies valid configuration.

Read Google Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google’s privacy policy for the services involved.

Your controls

Core gameplay works offline. In-game settings control music, effects, motion and haptics. Android advertising and privacy controls are managed through your device and the consent/privacy options exposed by the app when applicable. Optional rewarded recovery requires an explicit choice; ordinary retry remains available.

Support and release preparation

Prototype feedback can be recorded in the project issue tracker. Do not post personal information, device identifiers, account credentials or private save files in public issues. The owner must supply a monitored support contact and publish the finalized privacy policy before distribution.

Prepared 14 November 2026 for the Cinderbrook Brigade prototype. No store declaration has been submitted.
