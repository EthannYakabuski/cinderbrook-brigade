The concept is Cinderbrook Brigade: a warm, miniature 3D town where one satisfying gesture saves a neighborhood. Hold and drag a visible water reticle across glowing hotspots; lift to refill the water tank. Heat visibly falls, flames collapse into steam, wet objects glisten, and a clean green ring confirms safety. A fixed oblique camera, generous aim area and offset touch reticle keep play legible under a thumb. The player never has to navigate a character or aim a weapon. This is a stylized property-rescue arcade game, not real firefighting instruction.
